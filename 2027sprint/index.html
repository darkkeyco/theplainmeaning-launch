FAQ

Your questions, answered plainly.

How much time will this take?+

Plan on about 90 minutes a night, five nights. That's roughly an hour of teaching and workbook time plus up to 30 minutes of general Q&A. Add an action step in between, like a filing or a phone call. Very doable.

Do I need to have started my business already?+

Nope! Bring your idea and your questions. Already started? Great. This is your chance to catch anything you skipped.

Will we form my LLC during the sprint?+

No. I'll walk through sample articles of organization for a made-up business so you can see how the form works. Filing names and requirements differ by state and by type of business, and some businesses don't need a state filing at all. If you're ready, you file on your own between night 1 and night 2.

Does this cover my state?+

Yes. The sprint is for business owners anywhere in the U.S. I teach the general picture and point out where states tend to differ. Each action step has you look up your own state's rules, and I'll show you where.

Can I ask about my own situation?+

Please don't share the details of your situation. Q&A stays general, and I'll tell you when a question needs your own attorney. Send general questions ahead of time and I'll answer them for everyone.

Will you look at my contract or my website?+

No. You'll get a contract-reading checklist and a website checklist so you know what to look for. For a review of your own documents, talk to your own attorney.

Are the sessions recorded?+

Yes! Every session is recorded, and you have access to the replays for 6 months.

Is this legal advice?+

No. The sprint is general legal education. It doesn't create an attorney-client relationship. Laws and requirements depend on where you are and what you do, so for guidance about your business, talk to your own attorney or another qualified professional.
