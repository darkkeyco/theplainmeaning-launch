Fair questions

Is this legal advice?
No. It's general education, the questions most people should ask about most agreements, and it can't tell you what to do about yours.
Does it work in my state?
The questions apply anywhere. State law can change what the right answer is, which is one more reason to have a lawyer in your state look at anything big.
Can I send you my contract to look at?
We don't review documents or answer questions about specific situations. If you need someone to read yours, most state bar associations run a lawyer referral service. That's a good place to start.
