Privacy

Effective October 20, 2026.

This page explains what we collect. It is not legal advice, and nothing you send us creates an attorney-client relationship.

Who we are

The Plain Meaning is a legal education brand owned by Cabinet 13, LLC, a South Carolina limited liability company in the United States. On this page, "we" and "us" mean Cabinet 13, LLC. You can reach us at Tara Nixon, PO Box 154, Lugoff, SC 29078, USA, or at info@theplainmeaning.com.

Our sign-up forms ask only for your email. Kit sends a confirmation email first, and you're not on any list until you click to confirm. We keep the record of that confirmation as proof you asked to hear from us.

No cookies, analytics, or ad trackers. This site doesn't set cookies, run analytics, or carry advertising trackers. Our fonts are stored on our own server, so loading a page doesn't send your information to Google or any other font service. When you click "Put me on the list," you briefly pass through Kit's servers, which follow Kit's privacy policy.

Tracking across other sites. We don't track you across other websites, and we don't let anyone else use this site to do that.

Do Not Track. Because we don't track visitors across sites, a browser's Do Not Track signal doesn't change anything here. We treat every visitor the same way.

Please don't send us details about a legal problem. What you send isn't confidential or privileged, and we can't answer questions about your situation.

Why we use it

To send what you signed up for, like dates for a training or a new lesson. You can stop those emails anytime by unsubscribing.

To reply when you email us.

To keep the site running and secure, using the host's server logs.

We don't sell or rent your information, we don't share it for anyone else's advertising.

Information sent to the United States

We're based in the United States, and so are the services we use. If you're outside the US, your information goes to the US when you sign up or visit. Kit states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Trainings with Calling to Clarity

Some trainings are run with Calling to Clarity, another brand of Cabinet 13, LLC. When you register on callingtoclarity.com, that registration follows the Calling to Clarity privacy policy, and the sign-up page tells you which emails you'll get.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address and its confirmation record while you're subscribed. When you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you, and we delete your address within 12 months unless you ask us to delete it sooner. We keep emails you send us for up to 3 years. Our host keeps server logs on its own schedule.

Your rights and choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link that works without logging in. You can also ask us to show you what we have about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send you a copy. Write to us at the address above and we'll answer within 30 days. If your state or country gives you other privacy rights, we'll honor them as that law requires.

Children

The Plain Meaning is for adults running businesses. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has signed up, tell us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use reputable services and reasonable safeguards, and no system is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email address, we'll also tell subscribers by email before it takes effect.
